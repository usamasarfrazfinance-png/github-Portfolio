Case Study 04 · Philanthropy / Investment

Blue Meridian Partners

A full Power BI dashboard suite replacing manual spreadsheet tracking for a philanthropic investment fund managing over $1.2B in active and inactive commitments.

Role: Power BI / CRM Consultant Environment: D365 CE + Power BI
Power BIDynamics 365 CEDAXSSRSData Modelling

Overview

Blue Meridian Partners tracks investments across several philanthropic sub-funds: Nationwide Solutions, Place Matters, Justice & Mobility, The Studio, and Emergency Response. Reporting was previously scattered across spreadsheets. I built a connected Power BI dashboard suite covering leads, opportunities, prospects, investees, and investments, all pulling from the same underlying CRM data.

Investee and investment reporting

I designed drill-down dashboards for investee and investment tracking, with fiscal-year and period slicers, active-versus-exiting investment views, and fund-level breakdowns by total dollars, average dollars, and investment count, so leadership could see fund performance at a glance instead of pulling numbers manually each reporting cycle.

Working around a platform limit

Partway through the build, the client needed a closed-opportunities-by-quarter visual on a native D365 system dashboard. The system dashboard chart library in D365 only supports a fixed set of chart types, so that particular visual couldn't be built there no matter how the underlying view was configured. I identified the constraint, explained the trade-off clearly to the client rather than trying to force a workaround, and rebuilt the visual in Power BI instead, where the chart type, data, and formatting are all fully under our control.

Publishing and access

Finished reports were published directly to the CRM service, giving the fund's leadership real-time visibility into pipeline and investment activity without anyone having to generate or circulate a report manually.

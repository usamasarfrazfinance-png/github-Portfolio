Banking / Core Systems

Dynamics 365 CRM Modernization – Commercial Bank of Qatar

My Role: Dynamics CRM Developer on a highly customized, on-premises Dynamics 365 deployment wired directly into the bank's core banking system, responsible for the platform migration, plugin consolidation, and troubleshooting across onboarding, customer cases, and backend integrations.

The Challenge

The CRM had grown into a heavily customized, legacy-heavy system tightly coupled to core banking. Customer onboarding alone triggered more than 40 JavaScript functions on every form load, multiple plugins were registered on the same message and trigger, and existing integrations ran through an ESB layer that needed to move to KONG, all while the bank needed to migrate the platform from CRM v8 to v9 without disrupting live banking operations.

What I Changed

I led the end-to-end v8 to v9 migration, consolidated the onboarding JavaScript into shared reusable logic, merged overlapping plugins into components with conditional business rules, and migrated core banking integrations from ESB to KONG without breaking existing banking processes.

How I Worked

I audited the legacy implementation end to end, tracing processes through JavaScript, plugins, workflows, and Business Process Flow stages to find where they were actually failing. I worked past the CRM layer into stored procedures and SQL directly, and built reconciliation queries comparing the old and new environments so the team could validate configuration changes systematically during migration and server moves.

Business Outcome

The migration was delivered on schedule with zero unplanned downtime. Consolidating the onboarding logic cut form-load execution and improved maintainability, and merging redundant plugins reduced execution overhead and the risk of conflicts between different pieces of server-side logic.

Before

Legacy and fragmented

  • 40+ JavaScript functions firing on every onboarding form load
  • Multiple plugins registered on the same message and trigger
  • Core banking integrations running through a legacy ESB layer
  • Manual, ad-hoc comparison of environments during migration
After

Consolidated and validated

  • Onboarding logic consolidated into shared, reusable functions
  • Overlapping plugins merged with conditional business logic
  • Integrations routed through KONG
  • SQL-based reconciliation reporting for migration validation

Sales / Compliance / Commercial Operations

Dynamics 365 Sales Transformation – EQS Group (Germany)

My Role: I worked as Developer / Project Lead, partnering with business users to understand how opportunities, pricing, contracts, approvals, and customer data were being handled, and then helped turn that into a more structured, automated, and connected Dynamics 365 Sales solution.

The Challenge

EQS operates in a business where customer data, approvals, compliance checks, and structured communication are important. Sales teams needed a better way to manage opportunities, pricing, contracts, and related customer actions without relying on disconnected steps and repeated manual work.

What I Changed

I helped reshape the sales process inside Dynamics 365 so the system could support day-to-day commercial work more effectively. This included clearer process stages, better controls around data and approvals, and stronger integration with the external systems the business depended on.

How I Worked

I gathered requirements directly from business users, reviewed where delays and manual checks were happening, and proposed practical improvements. I then implemented automation for approvals, notifications, validations, and document generation, while also supporting integrations for company risk screening, VAT validation, HubSpot synchronization, and daily data movement to external systems.

Business Outcome

The new setup improved sales visibility, reduced manual work, and gave the business more control over pricing, contracts, validations, and customer data. It helped shorten the sales cycle by 25%, improved data consistency, and created a more reliable platform for sales and commercial operations.

Philanthropy / Reporting

Power BI & SSRS Reporting Suite – Blue Meridian Partners

My Role: Built a connected Power BI and SSRS reporting suite tracking leads, opportunities, investees, and investments across a philanthropic fund's sub-funds.

The Challenge

Reporting for a fund managing over $1.2B in active and inactive commitments across five sub-funds (Nationwide Solutions, Place Matters, Justice & Mobility, The Studio, Emergency Response) was scattered across spreadsheets rather than a single connected system.

What I Changed

I built drill-down dashboards for investee and investment tracking, with fiscal-year and period slicers and fund-level breakdowns by total dollars, average dollars, and count, replacing manual spreadsheet tracking.

How I Worked

When the client needed a closed-opportunities-by-quarter visual that ran into a real D365 platform limit (the native system-dashboard chart library only supports a fixed set of chart types), I identified the constraint, explained the trade-off clearly instead of forcing a workaround, and rebuilt the visual in Power BI, where the chart type and data are fully controllable.

Business Outcome

Leadership gained real-time visibility into pipeline and investment activity, with Power BI dashboards and SSRS reports published directly to the CRM service instead of relying on manually generated updates.
